Data Transparency

Data & Methods

Community Need

A standardized composite of poverty rate, rent burden (paying ≥30% of income on rent), and renter share per census tract (2022 ACS 5-year estimates). Higher values indicate greater housing-instability risk.

Service Access

An Enhanced 2-Step Floating Catchment Area (E2SFCA) score: resources reachable within a 15-minute travel catchment, weighted by competing household demand, percentile-ranked 0–100.

Service Gap

Community Need minus Service Access (both standardized). Positive values mean need exceeds access. Statistically significant clusters are identified with Local Indicators of Spatial Association (LISA) at the 5% significance level — shown on the map as "Service Gap Clusters."

Resource network

182 shelters, food pantries, clinics, legal aid, and social-service locations across Nassau & Suffolk counties, compiled from county and nonprofit resource lists.

Availability (prototype)

Beds, meals, wait times, and open/closed status shown throughout ReliefGrid are simulated for a subset of resources, or entered through the demo Provider Updates page. See services/availability.js for the adapter architecture a real integration (provider APIs, HMIS, Bitfocus Clarity, 211, findhelp, Unite Us, government open data) would plug into.

Limitations

  • Travel-time catchments are estimated, not live routing.
  • Availability is demo data unless a provider has updated it in this browser session.
  • Eligibility rules are not modeled per resource — always confirm with the provider.
  • McKinney-Vento school data (used in early research) reflects school-identified homelessness only; adults are undercounted.
Disclaimer: ReliefGrid is a prototype for research, demonstration, and planning discussion. It does not replace contacting a provider directly, and it does not represent a live operational system.
